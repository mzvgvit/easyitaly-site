How It Works

Way to the portfolio — five steps

Introduction

You send information about the company and the product line: assortment, certificates, production photos.

Evaluation

We assess quality, export potential and compliance with the requirements of target markets.

Commercial terms

We agree on the format of cooperation: distribution, FCA/FOB terms, minimum volumes.

Documents & logistics

We prepare the export documentation and choose the optimal transport scheme.

Launch & growth

Pilot shipment, distribution development and expansion into new countries.

Requirements

What matters to us

  • Consistent product quality and a stable production process
  • HACCP / ISO certification and readiness for export documentation
  • Capacity for regular export volumes
  • Competitive pricing policy for the target market
  • Readiness for long-term partnership and brand building
