How it works

The path into the portfolio — five steps

Introduction

You share company information and the product line: assortment, certificates, production photos.

Assessment

We assess quality, export potential and compliance with the target markets' requirements.

Commercial terms

We agree on the cooperation format: distribution, delivery terms, minimum volumes.

Documents and logistics

We prepare export documentation and choose the optimal transport scheme.

Launch and growth

Trial shipment, distribution development and expansion into new countries.

Requirements

What matters to us

  • Consistent product quality and a well-established production process
  • HACCP / ISO certification and readiness for export document flow
  • Capacity to deliver regular export volumes
  • Competitive pricing for the target market
  • Commitment to long-term partnership and brand development
