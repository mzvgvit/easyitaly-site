EasyItaly

From Tuscany to international markets

EasyItaly was founded in 2019 in the very heart of Tuscany — a region rightly considered one of Italy's gastronomic gems, famous for its culinary traditions, high product quality and culture of taste.

From the very start, our mission has been to introduce the international market to the best Italian-made products. We began with food supplies to Asia and the CIS, connecting Italian producers with partners and consumers worldwide.

Thanks to a deep knowledge of the Italian market, reliable partnerships and a commitment to excellence, EasyItaly gradually grew into an international distribution company.

Today EasyItaly represents and distributes products of well-known Italian brands in countries around the world, helping them expand their international presence.

We are proud of our Italian roots, we value traditions and we believe that true quality needs no borders.
