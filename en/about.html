Our Story

EasyItaly

From Tuscany — to international markets

EasyItaly was founded in 2019 in the very heart of Tuscany — a region rightly considered one of Italy's gastronomic jewels, renowned for its culinary traditions, high product quality and culture of taste.

From the very beginning our mission has been to introduce international markets to the best products of Italian manufacture. We started with food supplies to Asia and the CIS region, connecting Italy's producers with partners and consumers around the world.

Thanks to deep knowledge of the Italian market, reliable partnerships and a drive for excellence, EasyItaly gradually grew into an international distribution company.

Today EasyItaly represents and distributes products of renowned Italian brands across several countries, helping them expand their presence on international markets.

We are proud of our Italian roots, we cherish traditions and we believe that true quality needs no borders.
