Partnership models

EasyItaly partners can be importers and distributors, chain stores, supermarkets and trading groups — we tailor the cooperation format to your business.

Who we supply

Who can be a partner

The Italian assortment works at every level of retail — from a chain shelf to a standalone deli. We match the supply scheme to your format.

🛒

Retail chains & chain stores

National and local chains: a direct supply contract for the Italian assortment, plus support with new listings and promo launches.

🏪

Supermarkets & gourmet shops

Standalone supermarkets and premium delicatessens: shipments from trial batches and consolidated containers — no large minimum volumes.

🏢

Trading groups & buying alliances

Trading houses, buying groups and holdings operating several sites or countries: one contract and consolidated logistics for all of them.

🚢

Importers & distributors

The classic distribution model: territorial exclusivity, a framework agreement, marketing support and work with retail.
